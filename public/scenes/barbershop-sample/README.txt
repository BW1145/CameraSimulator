33 cabinet objects, six opaque material groups. Mirror and glass surfaces remain
outside this bake. Base colour is 2048px; roughness, tangent normal and indirect
light are 1024px. The GLB embeds PBR maps; indirect.png is loaded separately.
Original PNG bake outputs are retained for inspection and resumable generation.

The source camera's native optics are preserved in the sample. They do not
identify leocb's modified camera or missing desktop-fan model. Full source-light
recovery, reflections, classic fan composition and overall appearance are still
required before this sample can replace the production barbershop.
